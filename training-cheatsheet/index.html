STEP 1

Story card to RACE prompt

Fill in your story card on the left. The prompt on the right updates as you type, and you can edit it directly too. Empty boxes stay as [brackets], and a letter stays dashed until it has an answer.

  1. As a [user]→ R: Role (AI persona and user)
  2. I want to [action]→ A: Action (what it does)
  3. So that + Given [data]→ C: Context (data and limits)
  4. Done when + DoD→ E: Expectation (result and rules)
RRole who the AI acts as and who the app is for
Tells the AI who it is building for. Write one job title, not "everyone".
AAction what the app must do, in one sentence
The one thing they do on the screen. Start with a verb. One story, one action.
CContext data, sample files, reference info and limits
Why it matters to them. The AI uses it to decide what to put first; you test the build against it.
The files you give the app. Name them and their columns, so the AI does not make up its own data.

Name what is on screen and the fields it shows.
Where it shows up on the screen.

STEP 4

Fix prompts

When the build is off, send one of these. One change per prompt. Replace the [brackets] first.

Do

  • Cover Role, Action, Context and Expectation
  • Ask for one change per prompt
  • Say what you see and what you expected
  • Use fictional sample data in the prompt
  • Ask the app to flag missing or uncertain information

Avoid

  • Asking for "an AI app for everything"
  • Piling many changes into one prompt
  • Pasting confidential data into the prompt
  • Adding features not on your backlog
  • Trusting the output without a human check

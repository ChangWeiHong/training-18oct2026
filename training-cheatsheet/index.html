STEP 1

Notepad: fill in, then copy

Each box fills one [bracket] in the prompt. The grey hint under each label says why the AI needs it, then what to write. The prompt on the right updates as you type, and you can edit it directly too.

Tells the AI who it builds for, so it uses their words and shows the detail they need. Write one job title, not "everyone".
Tells the AI what problem the screen solves. Every part of the app should serve this. Start with a verb and add the benefit: "so they can ...".
Tells the AI what kind of data to make up for the samples. Name the readings or records, not a file type. The app ships with samples, so nobody uploads a file.
Gives the AI test cases to build, and gives you a way to check its rules. Write 3 lines: a name, then what you would see in the data (symptoms, not a label). Easy case, in-between case, worst case. Each line becomes one option in the dropdown.
Tells the AI what to do with the data. Pick one or two.
Sets the layout. Tables suit comparing, cards suit a quick status check.
Tells the AI exactly what to put on screen, so it does not guess. Name the columns or fields.
The person who checks the output. The app shows a note asking them to review it.
The real-world decision the app must not make alone. It goes into the review note.
Stops the AI adding extras you did not ask for, so the first build stays small and quick to check. One per line. Edit freely.
